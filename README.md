# Product Families

## Planetary Robotic Joint Modules

### SG6010C

Compact robotic joint actuator.

Applications:

* Exoskeleton Systems
* Humanoid Robots
* Service Robots

### SG8021

High-performance robotic joint actuator.

Applications:

* Humanoid Robots
* Quadruped Robots
* Industrial Robotics

## Cycloidal Robotic Joint Modules

### CPM80-25

Compact cycloidal robotic actuator.

Applications:

* Robot Arms
* Exoskeleton Systems
* Educational Robots

### CPM100-25

High torque cycloidal robotic actuator.

Applications:

* Humanoid Hip Joint
* Humanoid Knee Joint
* Collaborative Robots
